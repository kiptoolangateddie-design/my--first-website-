Album Showcase

Boldy James & The Alchemist - The Price of Tea in China (2020)

Album Overview

Released in 2020, this project is widely considered a modern underground classic. Produced entirely by The Alchemist, the album features cold, slow-tempo, dusty drum loops paired perfectly with Boldy James's gritty storytelling.

Standout Tracks

Here are some of the most popular tracks on the album rotation:

  1. Surf & Turf (feat. Vince Staples)
  2. Scrape the Bowl (feat. Benny the Butcher)
  3. S.N.O.R.T. (feat. Freddie Gibbs)
  4. Giant Slide
